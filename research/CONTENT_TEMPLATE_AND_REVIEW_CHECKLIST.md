This template is a gate, not permission to draft or publish. Reddit research and SEO Strategy Director approval come first.

## Required article structure (adapt to the real task; do not reuse mechanically)

1. Give a direct answer to the specific question.
2. Explain what evidence is verified, user-reported, inferred, and Unknown.
3. Provide steps or a checklist derived from the actual pain point.
4. Use a real CuriCart product/page example only when the current record supports it.
5. State risks, limitations, and what the reader must verify directly.
6. Add only related FAQs supported by research.
7. Add natural links to relevant products, categories, spreadsheet, QC finder, or the Primary Owner page.
8. End with `Sources`, `What we verified`, and `What remains unknown`.

## Reddit evidence review

- [ ] Every demand claim has public source URLs and concise summaries, not copied posts.
- [ ] “High frequency” has multiple independent sources or a second demand source.
- [ ] Community opinion is not presented as a platform fact.
- [ ] Deleted/private/login-bypassed material is absent.

Review:

- [ ] Official Embed/oEmbed only; no download, editing, rehosting, or copied thumbnails.
- [ ] Video is directly relevant, not filler, and does not autoplay.
- [ ] Responsive 16:9 reserved space and lazy loading prevent CLS.
- [ ] YouTube uses `youtube-nocookie.com` where compatible; TikTok uses official oEmbed.
- [ ] Page remains useful if the video disappears and retains a source link.
- [ ] No VideoObject/video sitemap unless the verified video is the page's primary above-the-fold content.

## Cross-site originality review

- [ ] Primary Owner is unique for this core intent.
- [ ] Evidence combination, user task, examples, structure, conclusion, and value differ materially from the other site.
- [ ] Headings/FAQ/CTA/screenshot and product-example combinations are not copied or rearranged.
- [ ] Draft is not a translation, summary, paraphrase, or platform-variable substitution.
- [ ] Duplicate heading check passed.
- [ ] Exact 8+ word cross-site sentence check passed, with legal/name exceptions documented.
- [ ] Human reviewer confirmed the page is not a rewrite; no originality percentage is used as the conclusion.

## SEO and factual release review

- [ ] Independent Title, Meta, H1, and search intent.
- [ ] Platform facts come from current official pages; unresolved facts say `Unknown`.
- [ ] No invented fee, shipping, policy, authenticity, inventory, discount, service, QC, or timeline claim.
- [ ] Schema exactly matches visible content.
- [ ] Content Growth Editor review completed.
- [ ] SEO Strategy Director approval recorded.
- [ ] Publication remains blocked until both reviews pass.
