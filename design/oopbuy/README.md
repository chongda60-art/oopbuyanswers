# Oopbuy design confirmation package

Status: visual concepts only. These images are not implemented pages and do not authorize coding or publication.

## Superseding final-direction concepts

1. `06-final-direction-desktop-home-questions-v3.png` — corrected public pre-launch homepage with no empty Topic links and approved/published-only Questions library.
2. `07-final-direction-question-topic.png` — internal QC candidate preview and unpublished Topic state; no fabricated product cards.
3. `08-final-direction-mobile.png` — mobile public pre-launch home and internal candidate detail.
4. `09-final-direction-video-states.png` — mutually exclusive no-video, click-to-load, unavailable-video and mobile states.
5. `10-final-direction-system-states-v2.png` — no logging/submission, Hold, hidden products, image failure, and archive decision branching to three mutually exclusive outcomes.

Earlier `01`–`06-v2` and first `10` concepts are superseded by `06-v3`, `07`–`09`, and `10-v2`. They remain for design history and must not drive implementation.

All concepts were generated with the built-in Image Gen tool from the approved Oopbuy research-tool brief. The final prompt set required a warm-white editorial research system, question search first, visible evidence roles, no Oopbuy logo, no official/authorized claim, no copied reference-site visual system, fixed video states, and explicit Unknown placeholders.

## Content caveat

The images define layout, hierarchy, component families and states. They do **not** verify any Oopbuy operation. The QC question is shown only as an internal Hold candidate. Public designs either use a neutral `[Approved question title]` sample or the true pre-launch empty state. No product placeholder is authorized for public output.

## Rejected drafts

The `rejected/` folder preserves four design-process drafts that contain invented dates, prices, counts, unrelated examples or operational wording. They are explicitly excluded from implementation and should not be presented for approval as final designs.

## Confirmed design decisions

- Warm-white/navy/coral direction.
- Restrained serif headings with sans body and controls.
- Home search hierarchy and Questions-first navigation.
- Old spreadsheet/QC routes merge into Topics.
- Video is click-to-load; no-video renders no module.
- Product previews are optional, task-relevant, API-backed, and capped at five.

Still pending: public brand name, domain, real CuriCart API contract, image hotlink permission/stability, caching, and product retirement semantics.
